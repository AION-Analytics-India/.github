# AION Lithuanian Tax Assistant · English

**Free public browser-based Lithuanian personal-income-tax calculator pilot for tax year 2026.**

[Live 2026 calculator](https://aionanalytics.in/lt-tax-demo.html) · [Lithuanian translation review](https://aionanalytics.in/lt-tax-review.html) · [Machine-readable English](llms.txt) · [Lietuviškai](README.lt.md) · [Lietuviškas llms](llms.lt.txt)

The demo invites 2026 income-by-type and selected Article 21 expense entries, shows cited statutory provisions, references GPM311 appendices, and offers a printable report. The page states that entered figures stay in the browser; no independent network or implementation audit is represented.

**Separate scope:** the public text-review form discusses filing obligations and deadlines. It is not proof that those Q&A features are implemented inside the 2026 calculator.

This is a pilot, not personalised tax advice, VMI electronic filing or a licensed-practice-approved paid service. Confirm the relevant GPMĮ edition and year with [e-Seimas](https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/) and [VMI](https://www.vmi.lt/). No proprietary software or data licence is granted by this documentation.
