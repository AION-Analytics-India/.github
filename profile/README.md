# AION Analytics (India)

**A guiding light, not a guiding hand. Information, not advice.**

AION Analytics (India) is a deep-tech research company building probabilistic models and the infrastructure to run them honestly, founded by Lokesh Gupta. We work in probabilistic forecasting, calibrated uncertainty, and execution-integrity systems — applied first to Indian financial markets, where the stakes make calibration and public validation unavoidable. The aim is narrow and deliberate: keep people from being harvested — in the markets they trade and in the data they generate. What we ship is measured, released with its failures visible, and built to inform rather than instruct.

## What we build

- **AION Veritas** — pre-open probability bands for NIFTY 50, BANK NIFTY, and SENSEX.
  Each band is published before the open and validated the same day in public, with
  misses kept on the record.
  → [aion-veritas](https://github.com/AION-Analytics-India/aion-veritas) ·
  [dashboard.aiondashboard.site/veritas](https://dashboard.aiondashboard.site/veritas)
- **Indian Market Intelligence** — macro-event and sector-impact analysis for Indian
  markets, derived from live news.
  → [GitHub](https://github.com/AION-Analytics-India/aion-indian-market-intelligence) ·
  [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/AION-Analytics/aion-indian-market-intelligence)
- **Indian Market Calendar** — open-source (MIT) session and holiday validation across
  NSE, BSE, MCX, and NSE Currency Derivatives.
  → [GitHub](https://github.com/AION-Analytics-India/aion-india-market-calendar) ·
  [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/aion-indian-market-calendar/)
- **Lithuanian Market Calendar** — the same idea in a second jurisdiction. Sixteen
  statutory holidays derived from first principles, so the package stays correct for
  any future year with no update.
  → [GitHub](https://github.com/AION-Analytics-India/aion-lithuanian-market-calendar) ·
  [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/aion-lithuanian-market-calendar/)
- **Execution integrity** — a device-resident chain-of-custody from signal to order.
  Patent-pending and deliberately undocumented in public.

## How the work is governed

Every model is held to the "Enki Protocol": evidence first, re-validate always,
output over operator. Results are published together with their failures — a claim we
cannot check is a claim we do not make. The proprietary methods behind our models,
including signal-provenance capture, are patent-pending and intentionally undisclosed
here.
